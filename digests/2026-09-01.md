## Extreme Weather

### Nepal-Tibet flood death toll passes 1,000

The toll from the August 26 Himalayan glacier-collapse flood crossed 1,000 on Tuesday, September 1, up from roughly 900 two days earlier. Nepal's National Disaster Management Authority put the national count at 675 dead and 2,498 still missing, with further deaths and missing reported across the border in Tibet. Officials said at least 11,814 people have been rescued so far since the flood struck.

### Tropical Storm Edouard forms in the Gulf, tracking toward the Texas-Louisiana border

A system that organized into Tropical Depression Five over the northern Gulf on Monday, August 31, strengthened into Tropical Storm Edouard and is forecast to make landfall near or just east of Galveston on Tuesday afternoon before moving inland over eastern Texas. Forecasters expect the strongest impacts along the Louisiana-Texas border, with 2 to 5 inches of rain and isolated totals of 6 to 9 inches, plus storm surge of 1 to 3 feet from High Island, Texas, to the Vermilion-Iberia Parish line in Louisiana. The system is expected to weaken and dissipate over land by early Thursday.
