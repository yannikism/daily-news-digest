## New AI Models

### Z.ai releases GLM-5.3-Flash, a 320B-parameter open-weight multimodal model

Z.ai released GLM-5.3-Flash on August 26, a 320B-parameter (18B active) mixture-of-experts model under the MIT license, with a 1,048,576-token context window and native image and video input. It's the first natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 family, combining sparse and linear attention, and had spent its prior week running anonymously as "Ox Alpha" on Chinese-made AI chips. Z.ai says it beats its predecessor GLM-5.2 at roughly a tenth of the price and lands within half a point of Claude Opus 4.8 on its internal coding benchmark, at $0.075 per million input tokens and $0.25 per million output tokens.

## Extreme Weather

### Southern California heat wave sets records dating back to 1870

Prolonged extreme heat is gripping Southern California through Friday, pushing inland valley and mountain temperatures as high as 112°F, with the National Weather Service saying the stretch could make this the hottest combined July and August in Los Angeles since records began in 1870. If forecasts hold, downtown LA would see triple-digit temperatures for three consecutive days for the first time since the 1880s.

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/southern-california-heat-wave-august-26) · CBS News Los Angeles

### Red flag fire warning covers northern LA County through Friday

A red flag warning is active until 3 p.m. Friday for the San Gabriel Mountains and the 5 and 14 freeway corridors in northern Los Angeles County, driven by high temperatures reaching 108°F, humidity below 10%, and wind gusts up to 30 mph. Local officials have added firefighting resources, opened cooling centers, and warned of possible power shutoffs.
