## New AI Models

### OpenAI launches GPT-Live-1 voice model in its API

OpenAI released GPT-Live-1 in the API on September 10, a full-duplex voice model that can listen and speak at the same time rather than chaining separate speech-to-text, reasoning, and text-to-speech steps. Voice sessions cost $0.05 per minute, billed per second, with backend model and tool usage billed separately; OpenAI is also expanding from a small set of real-time voices to a broader range of accents, dialects, and languages.

[Source](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-live-1-in-the-api/) · OpenAI

## Extreme Weather

### Lucas Fire grows to over 4,000 acres as red flag warning takes effect

The Lucas Fire on the Lake-Mendocino county line grew to 4,008 acres and 15% containment as of Saturday evening, September 12, up from 3,476 acres and 10% containment two days earlier. About 1,000 personnel are assigned to the fire, with air tankers flying suppression missions as conditions allow; a red flag warning is now in effect for the area through Monday afternoon due to gusty winds and low humidity, and evacuation warnings remain in place for parts of eastern Mendocino County and northern Upper Lake.

### Tropical Storm Norbert now forecast to dissipate before reaching Hawaii

As of 11 a.m. September 12, Norbert held 65 mph sustained winds about 1,935 miles east of Hilo, moving west at 14 mph. Forecasters had previously said it could strengthen into a hurricane approaching Hawaii, but by the September 12 evening outlook the storm was instead expected to weaken and dissipate well east of the islands by the middle of next week, with no coastal watches or warnings in effect.
