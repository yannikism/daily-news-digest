## Extreme Weather

### San Diego County under Extreme Heat Watch as records loom

An Extreme Heat Watch is set to begin Tuesday morning and run through Friday evening across San Diego County's coast, valleys and mountains, with coastal highs in the upper 80s to mid-90s and inland areas reaching the triple digits. Forecasters flagged potential daily temperature records at Ramona (105°F), Palomar Mountain (97°F), Campo (104°F) and Borrego Springs, with Palomar Mountain possibly tying or setting a record each day from Wednesday through Saturday.

### NHC tracking three Atlantic disturbances as development odds rise

The National Hurricane Center is monitoring three separate disturbances in the Atlantic basin heading into the season's most active stretch. The most notable is a tropical wave expected to move off the African coast early next week, which NHC gives a medium chance of organizing into a tropical depression by the end of next week; none of the three systems currently threaten the United States.
