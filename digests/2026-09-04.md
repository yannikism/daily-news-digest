## New AI Models

### OpenAI releases GPT-6 Astra, its first model to hit "Critical" cybersecurity capability

OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra on September 3, describing it as state-of-the-art on computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science and professional work. It is the first OpenAI model to meet the "Critical" capability threshold for cybersecurity under the company's Preparedness Framework, which OpenAI says triggered stronger safeguards ahead of release. Access is rolling out in phases: companies in OpenAI's application-based cybersecurity program get it first, with Pro, Plus, Enterprise, Business and API access following over the next week.

## Extreme Weather

### Nepal-Tibet flood toll revised sharply upward to 1,259 dead, over 5,000 missing

Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority put the toll at 1,259 dead and 5,083 missing in Nepal as of September 3, a jump from the 1,204 dead and roughly 3,900 missing reported the day before, with more than 5,000 others reported injured. In Tibet, Chinese authorities have recorded 21 dead and 541 missing. The disaster stems from flash floods and debris flows that struck the Trishuli River corridor after a landslide-triggered glacial lake failure on August 26.

[Source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nepal_floods) · Wikipedia (citing NDRRMA, September 3 update)

### Flash flooding hits NYC, Long Island and New Jersey with "life-threatening" warnings

Storms dropped up to 7 inches of rain on parts of New Jersey on September 3, undermining the shoulder of Route 322, while the National Weather Service issued flash flood warnings across Bergen and Union counties describing conditions as "life-threatening." Western New York saw a separate round of flash flood warnings through the evening, with 1.5 to 3 inches of rain reported in the Buffalo area, including Cheektowaga, Amherst, Tonawanda and Williamsville.
