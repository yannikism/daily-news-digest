### Two survivors pulled alive from Nepal flood wreckage 10 days after disaster

Rescuers freed Chinese hydropower worker Lu Haitao from 225 metres inside a flooded tunnel at the Trishuli hydropower project in Rasuwa district on September 5 — the third worker pulled alive from that site — while a Nepali woman was rescued from a collapsed house in Nuwakot and airlifted to Kathmandu for treatment. The rescues came as Nepal's death toll passed 1,331, with roughly 900 hydropower workers still unaccounted for, about 500 of them believed trapped in flooded tunnels across a dozen wrecked projects.

### Mudslide kills two, leaves 10 missing as Saudel's remnants keep flooding Fujian

A mudslide triggered by torrential rain from Typhoon Saudel's remnants killed two people and left 10 missing in eastern China. A dike overtopped and breached in Fujian, flooding villages and trapping residents, while Putian recorded 416.5mm of rain over Thursday and Friday — breaking the city's September daily-rainfall record, with some villages topping 500mm. About 600,000 residents have been evacuated from onshore and offshore risk areas in Fujian. China's Meteorological Administration declassified Saudel on September 5, though its residual circulation continues bringing heavy to torrential rain across Fujian, Jiangxi and Hunan.
