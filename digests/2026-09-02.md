## New AI Models

### NVIDIA releases Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550B-parameter open-weight model

NVIDIA debuted Nemotron 3 Ultra on September 1, a 550-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 55 billion active parameters per token, combining Mamba-2 state-space layers with selective attention layers across 512 experts (roughly 22 firing per token). The model ships with a 1-million-token context window, is released under NVIDIA's OpenMDW license permitting commercial use, and scores 48.0 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index — the top result among US open-weight models on that benchmark.

## Extreme Weather

### Nepal-Tibet flood death toll climbs to 1,114 as nearly 12,000 rescued

Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority put the death toll at 1,114 as of September 1-2, up from roughly 1,000 two days earlier, with about 4,500 people still unaccounted for on both sides of the border — including 592 foreign nationals and 933 hydropower-site workers. At least 16 people have died on the Chinese side. Nearly 12,000 people have been rescued in Nepal, and Prime Minister Balendra Shah said 279 workers have now been pulled from hydropower project sites; NDRRMA has mobilized 20,819 security personnel for the search, rescue and relief effort.

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/01/world/live-news/nepal-china-flood) · CNN

### Tropical Storm Edouard makes landfall near Texas-Louisiana border, weakens to depression

Edouard came ashore near Johnson Bayou, Louisiana at about 2:20 p.m. CDT with maximum sustained winds of 60 mph before weakening to a tropical depression over land. More than 71,000 Texas customers lost power, and downed trees blocked roads in Beaumont; the storm's main ongoing threat is heavy rain and flash flooding across southeast Texas and southwest Louisiana, with some areas forecast to see several more inches on top of what's already fallen.

[Source](https://www.foxweather.com/weather-news/tropical-storm-edouard-gulf-targets-texas-louisiana-landfall) · Fox Weather

### Severe storm threat returns to Great Lakes and Northeast, higher flood risk than Tuesday

The Storm Prediction Center's outlook for Wednesday, September 2 covers a repeat of Tuesday's pattern: morning storms near the eastern Great Lakes followed by a more concerning afternoon round that could include a few supercells capable of damaging winds, large hail and longer-lived tornadoes. Forecasters say Wednesday's flood risk is higher than Tuesday's because storms may hit the same ground already saturated by previous days' rain, part of a pattern of daily storm rounds that began Sunday and is expected to continue through at least Thursday, driven by a heat dome steering the jet stream north while baking areas farther south with record-challenging heat.

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/01/weather/storms-northeast-flooding-tornado-forecast-climate) · CNN
