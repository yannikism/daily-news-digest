## Climate Activism

### Protesters disrupt Nigel Farage's Reform UK conference speech over billionaire funding

Two women stood up during Farage's keynote at the Reform UK party conference in Birmingham, holding a banner reading "Reform works for billionaires" and chanting "billionaire bootlickers" before being removed by security. The group Climate Resistance said it was behind the disruption; separately, Greenpeace claimed responsibility for a banner reading "Collect £5 million, burn the planet" that appeared behind deputy leader Richard Tice during his own speech at the same conference.

## Extreme Weather

### Hurricane Lowell weakens to Category 1 as it passes Hawaii, warnings lifted

Lowell weakened from a Category 3 threat to a Category 1 hurricane with 80 mph winds as of 8 a.m. Tuesday, tracking 190 miles north-northwest of Lihue; the Hurricane Warning for Kauai County and Tropical Storm Warning for Oahu have both been cancelled. Kauai's mountains recorded nearly 18 inches of rain, with peak gusts of 85 mph at Waimea and 84 mph at Lihue, leaving severe damage and widespread power outages across Kauai and Oahu.

[Source](https://www.civilbeat.org/2026/09/hurricane-lowell-updates/) · Honolulu Civil Beat

### Extreme heat warning issued for Arizona-California desert border

The National Weather Service in Phoenix issued an Extreme Heat Warning on September 8 for Imperial County, Central La Paz and Parker Valley, and the Chuckwalla and Palo Verde valleys, effective until 8 p.m. on September 11. Forecast highs range from 102 to 114 degrees Fahrenheit across the warned area.
