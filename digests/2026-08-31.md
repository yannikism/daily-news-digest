### Nepal-Tibet flood toll nears 800 as rescuers reach trapped hydropower tunnel workers

The death toll from the August 26 Himalayan glacier-collapse flood climbed to nearly 800 across Nepal and Tibet as of Sunday, August 30, with more than 3,000 people still missing. Nepal's Independent Power Producers' Association said around 933 workers remain missing or unaccounted for at 11 hydropower project sites and tunnels in the Rasuwa and Nuwakot districts, among them citizens of Nepal, India, South Korea and China. Home Minister Sudan Gurung reported a rescue breakthrough late Saturday at the Trishuli Hydropower Project, where army teams opened a passage and began supplying oxygen to trapped workers; the Nepal Army has so far rescued 254 people from the Upper Trishuli-1 project and 18 from Langtang Khola, with officials saying more than 100 workers may still be alive in tunnels at the Trishuli 3A and 3B sites.

### Texas's Ross Fire grows past 85,000 acres, reaches 31% containment

The Ross Fire in Palo Pinto and Jack counties had grown to at least 85,000 acres and reached 31% containment by Sunday, August 30, up from 21% three days earlier. Crews battled multiple flare-ups on Saturday but kept them from crossing existing containment lines, and the fire is now North Texas's second-largest on record. Mandatory evacuations in Jack County remain lifted, though officials are asking Palo Pinto County residents to stay alert while the fire continues to burn near Possum Kingdom.

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/ross-fire-possum-kingdom-85000-acres-containment-super-scoopers-august-2026/) · CBS Texas

### Extreme Heat Watch issued for Kansas and Missouri as multi-day advisory follows

The National Weather Service issued an Extreme Heat Watch on August 30 for parts of southeast Kansas and Missouri, warning of heat index values up to 110°F from Tuesday afternoon through Friday evening. Separate Heat Advisories were issued the same day for the Kansas City and Springfield forecast areas, citing heat index values of 100–105°F and only limited overnight relief, with lows easing no lower than the mid-to-upper 70s.
