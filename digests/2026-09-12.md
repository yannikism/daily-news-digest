## New AI Models

### Sakana AI splits Fugu into Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2

Sakana AI released Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2 on September 11 — both are live through Sakana's OpenAI-compatible API. Fugu is an orchestrator model that routes each task to other models in a pool and stitches the results together rather than answering directly; Ultra v2 targets the hardest multi-step tasks and is priced at $5 per million input tokens, $30 per million output and $0.50 per million cached input (rising for contexts above 272K tokens). Sakana says Ultra v2 scores 48.3 on the Chartography visual-reasoning benchmark, versus 27.3 for Opus 5 and 29.5 for Fable 5, without using Fable 5, Fable 5.1 or GPT-6-Astra in its model pool.

[Source](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/10/sakana-ai-launches-fugu-max-and-fugu-ultra-v2-for-cheaper-stronger-multi-agent-orchestration/) · MarkTechPost

### DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash claims to beat its own flagship on cost and speed

DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash on September 10, a mixture-of-experts model with 552 billion parameters that the company says outperforms its larger V4-Pro flagship on performance, cost, speed and total runtime. It scored 90.6 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, narrowly ahead of Opus 5 (89.1) and GPT-5.6 Sol (88.8), while storing key-value cache in a 4-bit format that cuts its per-token memory footprint to about a quarter of V4-Flash's. Starting September 14, API requests to V4-Pro will be served by V4.1-Flash at the smaller model's rates until a V4.1-Pro version ships.

[Source](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/10/deepseek-releases-v4-1-flash-says-it-outperforms-flagship-v4-pro/) · SiliconANGLE

## Extreme Weather

### Wind-driven Lucas Fire spreads into Lake County, California, prompts evacuations

The Lucas Fire ignited Thursday morning near Potter Valley on the Mendocino-Lake county line and had grown to 3,476 acres and 10% containment by September 11. Evacuation orders cover eight zones in Lake County and two in Mendocino County, with evacuation warnings for eight more zones; Highway 20 is closed at Bachelor Valley, Witter Springs and Saratoga Springs roads, and a shelter has been set up at the Lake County Fairgrounds.

[Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/lucas-fire-mendocino-lake-counties-september-11-evacuations-ordered/) · CBS San Francisco

### Tropical Storm Norbert strengthens in East Pacific, still far from Hawaii

Norbert strengthened to 65 mph sustained winds and was centered about 1,870 miles east of Hilo as of September 11, moving west at 14 mph and forecast to reach hurricane strength this weekend. Forecasters say the track beyond that point is highly uncertain, hinging on how a mid-level ridge to the storm's north evolves, and that any potential Hawaii impact — if it happens at all — would be more than a week out.

[Source](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/09/11/breaking-news/tropical-storm-norbert-strengthens-in-east-pacific-as-trades-return/) · Honolulu Star-Advertiser

### Nepal seeks $20 million from UN loss-and-damage fund over glacial floods

Nepal formally asked the UN's Fund for Responding to Loss and Damage for more than $20 million, arguing the Rasuwa floods that followed the Langtang Lirung glacier and rock-ice collapse were driven by climate change. The floods have killed more than 1,300 people and left over 5,000 missing; Nepal's foreign minister said the sum requested is "obviously not nearly enough" against damage estimates that some put above $5 billion, and argued that high-emitting industrialized nations share responsibility for helping a country that produces under 0.1% of global emissions.

[Source](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/9/11/headlines/nepal_seeks_20_million_in_climate_compensation_after_glaciers_collapse_triggers_deadly_floods) · Democracy Now!
