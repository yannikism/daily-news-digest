## Extreme Weather

### Maricopa County's confirmed heat deaths jump to 113, with 584 more under investigation

Maricopa County's public health department now confirms 113 heat-related deaths in 2026 as of its August 22 count, up from the 81 confirmed reported earlier this week, with another 584 deaths still under investigation. Officials say most pending cases are ultimately confirmed as heat-related, and that roughly half of confirmed deaths involve substance use while a third occurred indoors, often without working air conditioning. The county says the toll remains on track to exceed last year's, despite expanded relief resources.

### Europe's back-to-back heatwaves linked to at least 35,000 excess deaths

Provisional figures reported by AFP and the EuroMomo monitoring platform put excess deaths from this summer's four record-breaking European heatwaves at 35,000 and up, with an AFP count across eight countries — Austria, Belgium, England, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Portugal and Spain — separately finding 33,000 excess or heat-linked deaths. EuroMomo's own mid-July-to-mid-August estimate stands at more than 32,000 excess deaths. Officials caution the figures are incomplete, covering only about half the continent, and will be revised over coming weeks.

[Source](https://www.irishtimes.com/environment/climate-crisis/2026/08/25/at-least-35000-excess-deaths-recorded-in-europes-back-to-back-heatwaves/) · The Irish Times

### Red Flag Warning issued for Santa Barbara County

The National Weather Service issued a Red Flag Warning for the Santa Barbara County interior mountains, adjacent foothills, and the Cuyama Valley, in effect from 10 a.m. Tuesday, August 25 through 3 p.m. Friday, August 28. The warning cites extreme heat, atmospheric instability, low humidity, and locally gusty winds, conditions the NWS says can produce rapid fire growth and extreme fire behavior, including plume-dominated fires.
