## Extreme Weather

### Typhoon Obet enters Philippine waters near super typhoon strength

Typhoon Saudel entered the Philippine Area of Responsibility at 1:20 p.m. Monday, Aug. 24, and was renamed "Obet." It has maximum sustained winds of 175 kph and gusts up to 215 kph, moving west-northwest at 30 kph, and PAGASA says it may still intensify into a super typhoon. The agency does not expect it to make landfall, forecasting it will exit PAR between Monday evening and Tuesday morning, though it is enhancing the southwest monsoon drenching northern and central Luzon.

### Human-caused Hawk Fire forces 42,000 to evacuate near Reno

The Hawk Fire, which ignited Saturday northwest of Reno, Nevada, had burned more than 15,000 acres and was 0% contained as of Sunday evening. Nevada Gov. Joe Lombardo declared a state of emergency and activated the National Guard, with 42,000 residents under mandatory evacuation orders and another 45,000 in warning zones. Six people, including three first responders, have been injured; officials say the fire was human-caused but have not said how it started.

[Source](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/23/us/hawk-fire-nevada-governor-emergency-reno-hnk) · CNN

### Extreme Heat Warning brings record highs to Texas and Oklahoma

An Extreme Heat Warning is in effect for North and Central Texas, with highs up to 109°F and heat index values of 103–113°F. Numerous daily record highs are expected through at least Tuesday across Texas and Oklahoma, including in Dallas and Oklahoma City, with afternoon temperatures reaching 100–110°F. A weak cold front is expected to bring slight relief later in the week.

## Climate Activism

### Thousands march in Stockholm weeks before Swedish election

More than 10,000 people marched in Stockholm on Sunday against government climate policy, joined by over 200 NGOs including researchers, youth and sports associations, trade unions, and religious and rights groups. The march comes weeks before Sweden's September 13 general election, in which 37% of respondents named climate their top issue. Organizers and outside experts say Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson's government — which has cut petrol taxes and eased low-carbon fuel requirements since taking power in 2022 — is jeopardizing the country's 2030 and 2045 emissions targets.

[Source](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260823-thousands-rally-in-sweden-to-protest-climate-policies-ahead-of-general-election) · France 24
