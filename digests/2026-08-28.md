## Climate Activism

### Maine activists rally against fossil-fuel legal immunity bill

About 50 people gathered at a noon rally in Portland's Lobsterman's Square on August 27, holding hand-painted signs in front of a giant inflatable oil barrel to oppose federal legislation that would shield oil and gas companies from lawsuits like the ones Maine plans to use to fund climate-damage recovery. A parallel rally was held in Lewiston. After about half an hour, the Portland group marched to congressional offices to deliver petitions signed by 1,100 residents; Third Act Maine co-facilitator Molly Schen told the crowd her father, a former Exxon executive, "would be horrified by the legacy that his company has left."

## Extreme Weather

### Texas's Ross Fire grows to 85,000 acres, evacuations lifted as containment improves

The Ross Fire near Palo Pinto and Jack counties, about two hours west of Fort Worth, has burned more than 85,000 acres and was 21% contained as of Thursday, August 27. The fire started near Ross in Palo Pinto County just before 1:30 p.m. Monday at an estimated 25 acres and exploded to more than 25,000 acres within 15 hours; roughly 630 households were evacuated at its peak, and mandatory evacuations along County Road 2210 toward Highway 281 in Jack County were lifted Wednesday morning as conditions improved. Three or four structures have been damaged, including buildings at Worth Ranch scouting camp in Palo Pinto.

[Source](https://fortworthreport.org/2026/08/27/public-asked-to-steer-clear-of-jack-palo-pinto-counties-as-crews-battle-wildfire/) · Fort Worth Report

### Phoenix hits 118°F, its hottest day of the year, as 2026 sets a warning-day record

Phoenix reached 118°F on Wednesday, August 26, the hottest day of the year and a record for the date, with the National Weather Service forecasting 114°F Thursday, 113°F Friday and 109°F Saturday under an Extreme Heat Warning that remains in effect through Saturday. Strong high pressure is keeping temperatures 5–9°F above normal and pushing the area into "Major to Extreme HeatRisk" territory; by Saturday, Phoenix is set to have logged 44 extreme heat warning days in 2026, the most to date for a single year.

### Tropical Storm Dolly forms in the Atlantic, expected to weaken before reaching the Caribbean

The National Hurricane Center designated Tropical Storm Dolly on Thursday, August 27, after the system developed a well-defined circulation and sustained winds of at least 40 mph roughly 1,555 miles east of the Leeward Islands. Dolly, the Atlantic season's fourth named storm, is moving west at more than 20 mph and is forecast to weaken into an open trough or tropical wave before reaching the northeastern Caribbean this weekend, bringing periods of heavy rain to the Virgin Islands, Puerto Rico and Hispaniola between Sunday and Monday with limited wind impact.
