## Extreme Weather

### Hurricane Lowell nears Hawaii's Niihau and Kauai as Category 2 storm

Hurricane Lowell strengthened impacts across western Hawaii as it passed roughly 135 miles southwest of Lihue Monday evening with sustained winds of 110 mph, on track to pass just west of Niihau and Kauai overnight into Tuesday morning. Niihau was already hit with 30-foot surf and offshore winds over 100 mph, thousands of Kauai households lost power, and a tornado watch was in effect for Kauai into early Tuesday. Forecasters warned of 6 to 10 inches of rain (isolated totals over 14 inches, with some areas possibly seeing up to 24 inches), flash flooding, mudslides and dangerous rip currents.

### Nepal holds national day of mourning as flood toll passes 1,380

Nepal held a national day of mourning on September 7, the 13th day since the August 26 glacial flood, with government offices at home and missions abroad flying the national flag at half-mast. The death toll from the Nepal-Tibet floods stood at at least 1,380 people killed and more than 5,500 missing.
