## Extreme Weather

### Category 3 Hurricane Lowell turns toward Hawaii, threatening flooding and dangerous surf

Hurricane Lowell, with maximum sustained winds of 125 mph, is forecast to pass roughly 100 miles west of Niihau (150 miles west of Kauai) from late Monday night into Tuesday morning, likely weakening to near Category 2 strength by its closest approach. Kauai County could see 6 to 10 inches of rain, with isolated totals over 14 inches, raising the risk of flash flooding and mudslides in steep terrain, while storm surge of 1 to 3 feet is forecast for Kauai. Surf is expected to build to 12–20 feet on Oahu and 12–16 feet across Maui County and the Big Island, with dangerous rip currents likely island-wide over the next several days.
