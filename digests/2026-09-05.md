## Extreme Weather

### Typhoon Saudel's third landfall floods Fujian, over 100 houses collapse in Putian

Typhoon Saudel, the 18th named storm of the season, made a rare third landfall in Zhangpu County, Fujian province, on September 3, with sustained winds of 23 metres per second. In Putian's Huating Town, more than 100 houses collapsed and multiple people were reported missing as floodwaters spilled over highways and damaged bridges; authorities evacuated 83,700 people from at-risk areas, and China's National Meteorological Centre recorded local rainfall totals up to 565.5mm (22.26 inches), with fifty rivers across Fujian exceeding warning levels. Meteorologists noted that three typhoons striking the same coastal subdistrict in a single year is unprecedented in China's recorded history.

### Nepal-Tibet flood toll rises to 1,287 dead in Nepal, over 5,300 injured

Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority reported on September 4 that the death toll had risen to 1,287, with more than 5,300 injured and 5,083 still missing, including 583 foreign nationals from 39 countries — up from the 1,259 dead reported a day earlier. In Tibet, Chinese state media reported 21 people killed and 541 missing, putting the combined toll from the August 26 glacial lake collapse at 1,308 dead and 5,624 missing.
