## Climate Activism

### Extinction Rebellion blocks The Hague's A12 highway again, two arrested

Extinction Rebellion activists walked onto the Utrechtsebaan off-ramps around 1 p.m. on August 29, blocking the A12 and creating a traffic jam as commuters tried to leave the city. The Hague's mayor, Jan van Zanen, ordered the group to clear the road immediately or be removed; two protesters were arrested for refusing to leave. The group is demanding an end to fossil-fuel subsidies, which it puts at €39.7–46.4 billion a year; an XR spokesperson told NOS that blocking the highway "is not forbidden" and that any resulting charges would be challenged in court.

## Extreme Weather

### Nepal-Tibet flood death toll passes 600, rescue resumes as barrier-lake risk eases

Nepal's death toll from last week's Himalayan glacier-collapse flood rose to 626 as of Saturday, August 29, with 2,426 people still missing on the Nepali side, 517 of them foreigners. In Tibet, Chinese authorities reported at least 7 dead and 554 missing. Rescue operations, which had been paused amid fears the barrier lake formed by the flood could breach, resumed after officials judged the lake's risk manageable; Nepal has asked India and China for specialist help to fill gaps in the search effort.

[Source](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/29/nepal-and-china-resume-flood-rescue-as-death-toll-nears-600) · Al Jazeera

### Extreme heat warning extended for California's Coachella Valley, records fall before Sunday cooldown

The National Weather Service extended an Extreme Heat Warning for the Coachella Valley, San Diego County deserts and San Gorgonio Pass through 8 p.m. on August 29, with highs in the lower 110s. Numerous locations broke maximum-low-temperature records overnight, including Palm Springs at 90°F, while Palm Springs' daytime high of 118°F tied a 2020 record and Indio and Thermal set new records. Forecasters expect a cooldown to begin arriving Sunday.
