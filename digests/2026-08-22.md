## Extreme Weather

### China raises Typhoon Maysak death toll to 159, citing dam collapses

Chinese authorities revised the flood death toll from July's Typhoon Maysak in Guangxi's Nanning and Guigang from 39 to 159, with 10 people still missing — the first update to the figures in six weeks. Officials said 107 of the deaths were caused by the collapse of the Liulan and Yunbiao reservoirs in Nanning. The flooding affected more than 1.65 million people, with cumulative rainfall exceeding 90 centimeters in the hardest-hit areas.

[Source](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/politics/article/3364814/chinas-typhoon-maysak-death-toll-soars-159-region-tallies-catastrophic-losses) · South China Morning Post

## Climate Activism

### Human Rights Watch urges governments to protect environmental defenders

HRW published a dispatch calling on governments to condemn violence against environmental and land defenders, adopt tailored protection plans, and coordinate with rights organizations, as part of its submission to the UN Special Rapporteur on the right to a healthy environment. The dispatch cites rising harassment and criminalization of Indigenous peoples and climate activists defending lands and ecosystems.

[Source](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/08/21/governments-should-protect-environmental-defenders-from-violence-and-repression) · Human Rights Watch

### UK rights group says anti-protest laws are silencing the climate movement

Liberty told Byline Times that the Police, Crime, Sentencing and Courts Act 2022, the Public Order Act 2023, and the newly passed Crime and Policing Act 2026 — which includes a ban on "cumulative disruption" — have significantly curtailed climate protest and direct action in Britain, with breaches now carrying unlimited fines. Liberty noted the Crime and Policing Act was rushed through before Lord Macdonald's independent review of protest and hate-crime legislation, commissioned by the Starmer government, was published.

[Source](https://bylinetimes.com/2026/08/20/where-are-the-climate-protests-human-rights-group-warns-government-crackdown-is-silencing-the-green-movement/) · Byline Times
