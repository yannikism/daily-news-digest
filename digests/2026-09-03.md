## New AI Models

### Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash and a Cyber security variant

Google released Gemini 3.8 Flash on September 2 — its third Flash release in six weeks — alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a variant optimized for cybersecurity tasks available only through Google Fairwind, a new limited-access program for governments and trusted partners. Google says 3.8 Flash brings substantial gains over 3.7 Flash in software engineering, agentic tasks and multi-step reasoning, at the same introductory price as 3.7 Flash — $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens through December 31, 2026. It's live now for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search and Gemini in Sheets, and for developers through Google's platforms.

[Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/3-8-flash-and-3-8-flash-cyber/) · Google Blog

## Climate Activism

### Just Stop Oil returns to Parliament Square, first street action in 17 months

Just Stop Oil protesters sat or lay in the road at Parliament Square in London on the afternoon of September 2, blocking traffic as new Prime Minister Andy Burnham held his first PMQs — the group's first action in 17 months. Around 30 protesters were on the road, with the group saying 60 people in total were blocking it; they were demonstrating against Burnham's suggestion of more North Sea oil and gas drilling, which the group says would do "nothing to lower UK energy bills or strengthen energy security." Police began making arrests within about 30 minutes, carrying protesters into vans.

[Source](https://juststopoil.org/2026/09/02/we-are-all-just-stop-oil-now-mass-arrest-outside-burnhams-first-pmqs/) · Just Stop Oil

## Extreme Weather

### Nepal-Tibet flood toll rises to 1,204, nearly 3,900 still unaccounted for

Nepal's National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority raised its death toll to 1,204 in a September 2 update, with 1,114 bodies recovered and roughly 3,900 people still out of contact; 11,993 people have been rescued in total. About 590 foreign nationals from 34 countries are among the missing, and international forensic and tunnel-rescue teams from India, China, South Korea and Singapore are assisting. Families of the missing are being asked to submit DNA samples to Nepal Police Hospital in Maharajgunj to aid identification of recovered remains.

[Source](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/world/nepal-flash-flood-death-toll-reaches-1204/amp) · The Tribune

### Edouard's remnants trigger flash flood emergency in East Texas, up to 28 inches of rain

The remnants of Tropical Depression Edouard dropped 15 to 28 inches of rain on Hardin and Tyler counties in East Texas by September 2, prompting a flash flood emergency and what forecasters described as considerable to locally catastrophic flash flooding. The National Weather Service's Weather Prediction Center placed the Big Thicket-to-Piney Woods region under a rare High Risk of excessive rainfall — a category issued on only about 4% of days but linked to roughly a third of US flood deaths and 80% of flood damage — with rainfall rates reaching 2 to 3 inches per hour.

[Source](https://weather.com/2026/09/02/news/weather/liveblogs/tropical-storm-edouard-remnants-not-done-with-texas) · Weather.com

### Flash flooding hits central Pennsylvania overnight

Thunderstorms dropped 4 to 7 inches of rain on parts of central Pennsylvania overnight into September 3, prompting the National Weather Service to issue flood warnings for Union, Clinton and Lycoming counties, with local law enforcement reporting flooding and road closures. Separate flood warnings were also issued for Erie County in the northwest and for Schuylkill, Dauphin and Lebanon counties in the south-central part of the state.
